Clipper

Clipper is a desktop app for creators and authorized clip editors. It turns long live-stream recordings into short vertical clips with captions and lets the creator review each clip and publish it to their own TikTok account.

How it works

  1. Paste the link of a live-stream recording you own or are authorized to clip.
  2. Clipper finds the highlights, cuts vertical clips and adds captions.
  3. Connect your TikTok account with Login Kit, review each clip and choose its privacy and interaction settings.
  4. Click "Post to TikTok". Nothing is posted without that action.

Clipper runs on the user's own computer. It does not add watermarks or promotional branding to the content.
