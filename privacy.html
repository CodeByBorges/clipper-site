Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026.

Data we access

  • From TikTok, with your permission: your open ID, nickname, username and the posting options returned by TikTok, used only to show which account will receive the post and to publish the clips you choose.
  • From the recordings you provide: audio, transcript and chat replay, used only to find the highlights.

Storage

Your TikTok access token and the generated clips are stored only on your own computer. We do not run a server that stores your data. We do not sell or share your data and do not use it for advertising.

Third-party services

Short transcript excerpts are sent to the OpenAI API to pick the highlights. Publishing uses the official TikTok API.

Deleting your data

Revoke Clipper's access in TikTok under Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services, and delete the local token file. This removes all stored data.
